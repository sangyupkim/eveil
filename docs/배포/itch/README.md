## 5. Description (그대로 복사해서 붙여 넣기 — itch 편집기에서 굵게 · 목록은 단추로 맞춘다)

**No classes. The way you fight shapes who you become.**

ÉVEIL is an isometric action RPG you play right in your browser — on PC or on your phone (landscape). There are no fixed classes: your character grows from what you actually do. Swing a blade and your Strength rises. Fire arrows for Dexterity, cast spells for Intelligence, endure hits for Vitality.

**The loop is simple:** fight to grow stronger → beat bosses and dungeons for better gear → enhance it → push into tougher lands.

**Features**
- **Grow by doing** — five stats that rise from how you fight. Each Awakening lets you pick a title and perk that fit your style.
- **Hundreds of skills** — fire, frost, lightning, blades, bows, holy and shields. Skills rank up as you use them, and you can mix two paths into hybrid skills.
- **One-handed or two-handed** — a wand with a tome for fast single-target spells, or a staff for slow, wide destruction. Sword and shield, or a greatsword. Every path has both.
- **20 regions, endless world tiers** — Peace, Nightmare, Hell, Chaos and beyond, each with its own bosses and dungeons.
- **Dungeons, hidden dungeons and floods** — random mutators every run, secret dungeons found from map fragments, and monster floods in lands you leave behind.
- **Loot** — rarities, affixes, gems, enhancement, reforging, named legendaries and mythic gear.
- **Your own village** — build a smithy, workshop, lab and guild hall, and hire mercenaries to craft, research and go on expeditions.
- **Auto or manual** — let auto-hunt grind for you, or fight yourself for better rewards.
- **Play together, alone** — a single-player game with live chat and level / power rankings.

**Controls**
- PC: Click or WASD to move · Space to attack · 1–8 skills · Q / E potions · F auto-hunt · R recall · I / K / C / J windows · Enter to chat
- Mobile: landscape mode, left joystick, right buttons

**Saving**
Your progress is saved in your browser. Create a free account in game to keep your characters on the server and continue on any device.

*Available in English and Korean (switch on the title screen).*
